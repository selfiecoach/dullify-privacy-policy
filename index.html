Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short

  • Dullify does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • Everything you set up stays on your phone.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

This policy explains how Dullify ("the app") handles information. Dullify is a calm, distraction-free home screen (launcher) for Android.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. Dullify does not request internet access, has no servers, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. No information about you or your use of the app is ever transmitted to us or to any third party.

Information stored on your device

To work, Dullify keeps a small amount of information locally, inside the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your setup: the modes you create, which apps appear on your home screen, and which apps should pause before opening.
  • Focus sessions: when a session started, how long it lasted and how it ended.
  • Pause history: when a pause screen was shown, for which app, and whether you went back or continued. This powers the Insights screen.
  • Preferences: such as your theme and current mode.

This information never leaves your device. Android cloud backup is turned off for Dullify, so it is not copied to your Google account either.

Permissions and why they are used

List of installed apps

As a home screen, Dullify needs to see which apps are installed so it can show and open them. This list is read on your phone and is not stored or shared.

Usage access (optional)

If you turn it on, Dullify reads how long apps you chose to pause were in the foreground. This is used only to show "time in paused apps" in Insights and for daily limits you set. The data is read from Android on demand and is not copied elsewhere or shared. If you leave it off, the app shows "No data".

Accessibility service, "App interruption" (optional)

If you turn it on, Dullify uses Android's Accessibility API only to notice which app has just come to the foreground, so it can show a pause screen before an app you chose to pause. It:

  • reads only the name of the app that opened;
  • cannot read what is on your screen. It is configured without permission to retrieve window content, so it never sees text, passwords, messages or anything you type;
  • does not perform actions on your behalf;
  • does not store or share anything beyond the local pause history described above.

Dullify asks for your consent with an in-app explanation before you enable it, and you can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility. Dullify works without it.

Notifications (optional)

Used only to show a silent note on your phone when a focus session ends.

Run at startup

Lets Dullify restore an active focus session timer after your phone restarts.

Exporting your settings

If you choose Export settings, Dullify writes a file with your modes and app rules to a location you pick. That file is handled by you. Dullify does not upload it anywhere.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with anyone, because we do not have any.

Deleting your data

You are in control at all times:

  • Settings → Clear usage history removes past sessions and pause history.
  • Settings → Delete all data erases everything Dullify has stored.
  • Uninstalling Dullify removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Dullify is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your information stays in Dullify's private app storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit or on servers to protect.
